# Sample transcript — source

`sample_transcript.txt` is the full text of Dwight D. Eisenhower's First
Inaugural Address, delivered 20 January 1953.

- **Source text:** The American Presidency Project,
  https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/inaugural-address-3
  (checked 20 November 2026; the page notes the text follows the White
  House release of the address). Cross-checked against the U.S. National
  Park Service,
  https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/eisenhower-s-1953-inaugural-address.htm
  — both print the same text.
- **Why it is public domain:** an inaugural address delivered by the
  President of the United States in office is a work of the United States
  federal government, which is public domain under 17 U.S.C. § 105. The
  address was also printed as a U.S. Senate document by the U.S.
  Government Printing Office (Senate Document No. 9, 83rd Congress, 1st
  Session). The same class of document is catalogued "Copyright Status:
  Public domain" by the John F. Kennedy Presidential Library for JFK's
  1961 inaugural address
  (https://jfklibrary.org/learn/about-jfk/historic-speeches/inaugural-address).
- **Edits:** none to the wording. One full stop was restored after
  "lightness against the dark", which both source pages print without
  terminal punctuation — without it the sentence parser merges two
  sentences. Line breaks are paragraph breaks from the source.
